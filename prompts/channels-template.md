# Marketing & Channels Deliverable Template

<!--
  This template maps to the Phase 2 Channel Strategy deliverable from AI_Prompt_Templates_Consolidated.
  Each section tells the extraction prompt what insights to surface.
-->

## 1. Current Marketing State

What marketing the company is doing right now — channels, frequency, what's working, what's not.

Extract:
- **Active channels** — every channel the founder mentioned they're currently using (email, social, events, ads, content, etc.)
- **What's working** — channels or tactics the founder said are producing results
- **What's not working** — channels or tactics the founder is frustrated with or would kill
- **Publishing frequency** — how often they're creating/publishing content now
- **CRM status** — what CRM they use, how many contacts, how organized it is

## 2. Goals & Success Metrics

What the founder wants to achieve and how they'd measure success.

Extract:
- **90-day success definition** — specific metrics or outcomes the founder named (leads, meetings, pipeline, awareness)
- **Primary goal** — the single most important marketing outcome
- **Secondary goals** — other objectives mentioned
- **Current gaps** — what's preventing them from hitting these goals now

## 3. Team & Resources

Who's involved in marketing and what resources are available.

Extract:
- **Team size** — how many people are involved in marketing
- **Roles** — who creates, who approves, who publishes
- **Budget for tools** — the rough range the founder gave
- **Constraints** — any resource limitations that affect channel strategy (time, skills, headcount)

## 4. Channel Preferences

Specific channels the founder wants to use or avoid, and their reasoning.

Extract:
- **Wanted channels** — channels the founder explicitly wants to be on, with reasoning
- **Avoided channels** — channels the founder wants to avoid, with reasoning
- **Niche channels** — any industry-specific or audience-specific channels mentioned (forums, Slack groups, communities, events)
- **Channel priority** — if the founder ranked or prioritized channels, capture the ranking

## 5. Strategic Context

Broader context that informs channel strategy — connects marketing reality to business goals.

Extract:
- **Buying cycle alignment** — how marketing channels should map to the buying cycle discussed in ICP
- **Content themes** — topics or themes the founder wants to focus marketing on
- **Competitive positioning** — how they want their marketing to differ from competitors
- **Quick wins** — any low-hanging fruit or immediate opportunities the founder mentioned
