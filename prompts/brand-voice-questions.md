# Section A — Brand Voice Discovery Questions (1–22)

## A1: Foundation
1. What does your company do? Give me the 30-second version. (Follow-up: If you had to explain it to someone at a barbecue, how would you say it?)
2. What are your core services or products? (Follow-up: What do people actually buy from you?)
3. What are your core values — the real ones, not the ones on the wall? (Follow-up: If I watched your team work for a week, what would I see that proves these values?)
4. Why does this company exist? What's the mission? (Follow-up: What would be lost if you disappeared tomorrow?)
5. Tell me about a specific client result you're proud of. (Follow-up: Walk me through what happened — what they came in with, what changed.)

## A2: Personality
6. If your brand walked into a room, how would people describe them? (Follow-up: What's the vibe? The energy?)
7. Give me 3–5 personality traits that define your brand. (Follow-up: Not values — personality. How you show up.)
8. What should your brand never sound like? (Follow-up: What's the anti-you?)

## A3: Tone & Style
9. On a scale of 1–10, how formal should your content be? (Follow-up: 1 = texting a friend, 10 = legal brief)
10. Do you use contractions? (Follow-up: "We're" vs "We are" — what feels right?)
11. Any slang or casual language that's on-brand? (Follow-up: Industry terms that are OK to use loosely?)
12. How do you address your audience? (Follow-up: "You," "our clients," "partners," something else?)
13. How technical should your language be? (Follow-up: Jargon-heavy or plain English?)

## A4: Scenarios
14. How would you announce a new feature or service? (Follow-up: Describe the tone and approach.)
15. How would you apologize for a service disruption? (Follow-up: What's the right balance of empathy and professionalism?)
16. How would you explain a complex technical concept to a prospect? (Follow-up: Jargon-heavy or simplified?)
17. How would you congratulate a client on a win? (Follow-up: Formal acknowledgment or enthusiastic celebration?)

## A5: Differentiation & References
18. Are there specific words or phrases you love using? (Follow-up: Anything that feels distinctly "you.")
19. Are there words or phrases to absolutely avoid? (Follow-up: Industry clichés, competitor language, etc.)
20. How do you want to sound different from your competitors? (Follow-up: What makes your voice unique?)
21. Name 2–3 brands whose voice you admire and why. (Follow-up: They don't have to be in your industry.)
22. What do your best clients say about working with you? (Follow-up: Use their words, not yours.)
