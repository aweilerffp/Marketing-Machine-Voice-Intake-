# Section C — Marketing & Channels Discovery Questions (34–41)

## C1: Current State
34. What marketing are you doing right now? (Follow-up: Email? Social? Events? Ads? Content? Nothing?)
35. What's working and what isn't? Be honest. (Follow-up: If you had to kill one channel tomorrow, which one?)
36. How often are you publishing anything right now?
37. Do you have a CRM? What's in it? (Follow-up: How many contacts? Are they segmented? Is it a mess?)

## C2: Goals & Resources
38. What does success look like in 90 days? (Follow-up: Be specific. More leads? More meetings? Pipeline? Brand awareness?)
39. How many people are involved in marketing? (Follow-up: Who creates, who approves, who publishes?)
40. What's your budget for marketing tools? Rough range.
41. Any channels you specifically want to be on? Or avoid? (Follow-up: Why? Bad experience? Audience isn't there?)
