# Section B — Ideal Customer Profile Discovery Questions (23–33)

## B1: Who They Are
23. What industry are your ideal customers in? (Follow-up: If you could clone your best client 10 times, what industry are they in?)
24. What size company? Revenue, headcount, whatever metric matters.
25. Who's the decision-maker? What's their title? (Follow-up: Who actually signs the contract?)
26. Who else is involved in the buying decision? (Follow-up: Is there a committee? A technical gatekeeper? A CFO who needs to approve budget?)
27. Where do these people hang out online? (Follow-up: LinkedIn? Specific forums? Slack communities? Reddit? Facebook groups? Where would you go to find 100 of them right now?)

## B2: Their Problem & How They Buy
28. What problem do you solve for them? In their words, not yours. (Follow-up: What are they Googling at 11pm when they're frustrated?)
29. Why do they choose you over the alternatives? (Follow-up: What did the last client who signed say about why they went with you?)
30. What were they doing before they found you? (Follow-up: DIY? A competitor? Nothing at all? Spreadsheets and duct tape?)
31. What happens if they do nothing? What's the risk? (Follow-up: What gets worse? What opportunity do they miss?)
32. Walk me through their buying cycle. (Follow-up: How long from first conversation to signed contract? Is it budget-driven? Who needs to approve it?)
33. What triggers them to start looking for a solution? (Follow-up: A specific pain point? A mandate from leadership? Growth? A failure?)
