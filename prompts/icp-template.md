# Ideal Customer Profile Deliverable Template

<!--
  This template maps to the Phase 1 ICP deliverable from AI_Prompt_Templates_Consolidated.
  Each section tells the extraction prompt what insights to surface.
-->

## 1. Primary ICP

The core ideal customer profile with specifics from the session.

Extract:
- **Industry** — the specific industry or industries the founder named
- **Company size** — revenue range, employee count, or whatever metric the founder used
- **Decision-maker role** — title/role of the person who signs the contract
- **Key pain points** — the problems these customers face, ideally in the customer's words
- **Geographic or market scope** — any regional, national, or market-specific constraints mentioned

## 2. Buying Behavior

How these customers discover, evaluate, and buy.

Extract:
- **Trigger events** — what causes them to start looking for a solution
- **Buying cycle length** — how long from first conversation to signed contract
- **Decision process** — who's involved, who approves budget, how decisions get made
- **Common objections** — what pushback the founder typically encounters
- **Before state** — what they were doing before (DIY, competitor, nothing, spreadsheets)
- **Risk of inaction** — what happens if they do nothing (what gets worse, what they miss)

## 3. Where They Live

Specific channels, communities, and spaces where this audience spends time.

Extract:
- **Online channels** — LinkedIn, forums, Slack communities, Reddit, Facebook groups
- **Events and publications** — conferences, trade publications, newsletters
- **Niche communities** — any specific, named communities the founder mentioned
- **Where to find 100 of them** — the founder's answer to "where would you find them right now"

## 4. Messaging Hooks

5-10 messages that would resonate with this audience, written to align with the brand voice. These should come from the founder's own framing of the value proposition, pain points, and differentiation.

Extract:
- **Pain-point hooks** — messages that speak to the customer's problem
- **Outcome hooks** — messages that speak to results and transformation
- **Differentiation hooks** — messages that position against alternatives
- **Trust hooks** — messages that leverage social proof or credibility markers

## 5. Secondary ICP

If the founder mentioned a secondary audience or customer segment, capture it here with the same level of detail. If not mentioned, this section can have minimal insights noting that no secondary ICP was discussed.

Extract:
- **Secondary segment** — who they are and how they differ from the primary ICP
- **Why secondary** — why they're a fit but not the primary focus
- **Key differences** — how messaging or approach differs for this segment
