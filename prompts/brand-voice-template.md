## 1. Voice Personality
How the brand shows up — its character, energy, and human traits. Extract personality descriptors, brand-as-a-person metaphors, and how the founder wants the brand to be perceived.

## 2. Core Values in Action
The real values driving the company — not wall art, but observable behaviors. Extract stated values, proof points (stories, examples), and how values show up in client work.

## 3. Content Pillars
The key topics and themes the brand should consistently talk about. Extract subject areas the founder is passionate about, areas of expertise they referenced, and recurring themes.

## 4. Tone & Style Cheat Sheet
The specific stylistic rules for how the brand communicates. Extract formality level, vocabulary preferences, technical depth, audience address style, and grammar/punctuation preferences.

## 5. Scenario Playbook
How the brand voice adapts to different situations. Extract approach for announcements, apologies, education, celebration, and other communication scenarios discussed.

## 6. Do vs Don't
The guardrails — what's on-brand vs. off-brand. Extract loved words/phrases, banned words/phrases, admired brand references, competitor differentiation, and explicit rejections.
